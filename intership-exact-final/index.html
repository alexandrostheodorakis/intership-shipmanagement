Our Solutions

Technical Services

With extensive experience in ship management, Intership Shipmanagement provides comprehensive technical management services for dry bulk carriers and tankers. Our technical team works closely with shipowners, vessels and crews to maintain high standards of safety, reliability and operational efficiency. Through proactive maintenance, careful planning and effective cost control, we help our clients optimise vessel performance throughout the entire operational lifecycle.

Daily Vessel Performance Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of vessel performance, fuel consumption, machinery condition and operational efficiency, allowing potential issues to be identified and addressed at an early stage.

Planned & Condition-Based Maintenance

Proactive maintenance planning combined with condition monitoring helps keep vessels safe, reliable and operationally efficient while avoiding unnecessary downtime and controlling maintenance expenditure.

Procurement & Technical Supplies

Efficient sourcing of spare parts, lubricants, technical stores, equipment and other vessel requirements through an established network of trusted suppliers and service providers.

Drydocking & Repair Projects

Comprehensive planning and supervision of drydocking, repair and refurbishment projects, from initial preparation and shipyard selection through to completion and final inspection.

Newbuilding Project Management

Technical supervision throughout the newbuilding process, including design review, construction monitoring, inspections, commissioning and delivery, with a focus on quality, schedule and cost control.

Digitalisation & Remote Monitoring

Modern digital tools and remote monitoring solutions provide greater visibility of vessel performance, maintenance activities and operating costs, supporting faster and better-informed decisions.

Operational Services

At Intership Shipmanagement, we provide comprehensive operational management designed to ensure the safe, efficient and reliable operation of dry bulk carriers and tankers. From voyage planning and cargo operations to regulatory compliance and performance monitoring, our team coordinates every aspect of vessel operations with a strong focus on safety, efficiency and cost control.

Safety, Compliance & Environmental Standards

We maintain a strong focus on safe operations and environmental responsibility, ensuring that vessels operate in accordance with applicable international regulations, flag-state requirements and industry standards.

Support for Masters & Crew

Our shore-based team provides continuous operational support to masters, officers and crew, ensuring effective communication between vessel and shore.
We assist with voyage requirements, operational challenges, documentation and coordination with agents, terminals, charterers and other parties involved in the vessel’s operation.

Cargo Operations

We coordinate cargo operations with particular attention to the specific requirements of each vessel and cargo.
Our experience covers a wide range of dry bulk cargoes, steel products, project and general cargoes, as well as tanker operations, with a focus on safe cargo handling, efficient port operations and minimising delays.

Voyage & Weather Optimisation

Modern routing and voyage-planning tools are used to support efficient navigation and operational decision-making.
Weather forecasts, routing information, vessel performance data and other relevant parameters are continuously monitored to help optimise voyages, improve fuel efficiency and reduce operational risks.

Performance Monitoring & Transparency

We maintain clear oversight of operational performance through regular monitoring of key performance indicators, bunker consumption, voyage results, delays, claims and other relevant operational data.
